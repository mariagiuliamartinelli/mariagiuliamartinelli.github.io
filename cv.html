Skills

What I actually do at the bench and in R

Osteology, Taphonomy & Conservation of Skeletal Remains

Skeletal inventory and element identification · biological profile (age, sex, stature) · dental palaeopathology · macroscopic assessment of bone preservation, weathering and taphonomic alteration · selection of elements and sampling loci · state-of-conservation assessment and collection management · photographic documentation and record-specimen traceability

Laboratory & Analytical Practice

Molecular: genomic and ancient DNA extraction · PCR (16S rRNA, ITS) · amplicon sequencing · sterile technique and contamination control in low-copy-number contexts.
Microscopy & diagnostics: optical and stereomicroscopy of heritage and biological materials · biofilm sampling and documentation

Data Analysis, Statistics & Reporting

R (tidyverse, phyloseq, vegan, ggplot2, sf, leaflet) · non-parametric testing · multivariate ordination (PCoA, Bray–Curtis, Procrustes) · publication-quality figures and tables · reproducible pipelines, Git/GitHub, LaTeX · written reports and manuscripts
