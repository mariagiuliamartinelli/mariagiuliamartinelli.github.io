Pipelines and tools I write and release

Reproducible analysis, openly published: from cranial collections to HPC classifier benchmarks.

R

Etruscan-Analysis: Bioarchaeological Pipeline

Pipeline for a historical cranial collection: record standardisation and auditing, anatomical heat maps of dental pathology and taphonomic damage, stress markers by age cohort, and interactive provenance mapping. Ships with a mock dataset for reuse.

tidyverse · sf · leaflet
R

Bioarchaeological-Systematic-Review

Framework for literature data mining and statistical analysis of osteological stress markers in non-adult populations: 28 stress indicators, co-occurrence matrices, chronological parsing of site intervals, and non-parametric hypothesis testing. Analytical backbone of the systematic review.

Non-parametric testing
R · Shell

kraken2-krakenuniq-comparison

R and shell scripts for the Valencia aDNA comparison on the Dardel HPC cluster: SLURM job arrays, abundance-matrix construction, cross-tool validation and pathogen screening.

SLURM · PDC / KTH
JavaScript

CO2 Time Machine

Interactive 3D globe for temporal and spatial mapping of environmental data. Developed for Communicating Environmental Science (MI4008), Stockholm University.

Stockholm University

Biofilm Metagenomics Workflow (Casino Algardi)
