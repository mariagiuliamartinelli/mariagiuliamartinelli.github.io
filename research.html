Laboratories, field sites and pipelines

Five research placements across palaeogenetics, osteology, heritage metagenomics and iconographic analysis.

04/2026 – 06/2026 Stockholm University / Sapienza University of Rome

Research Internship at the Centre for Palaeogenetics (CPG)

  • Analysed 250 ancient DNA libraries from Roman, Visigothic, Islamic and Medieval necropolises in Valencia (Spain), sampled from petrous bone, teeth and phalanges: elements with markedly different susceptibility to post-mortem alteration.
  • Ran Kraken2 (GTDB) and KrakenUniq (MicrobialNT) classification pipelines as SLURM job arrays on the Dardel HPC cluster (PDC, KTH), resolving the endogenous signal from the soil-derived community that records post-mortem colonisation of bone.
  • Assessed cross-tool agreement (Procrustes, Spearman, PCoA on Bray–Curtis) and screened all samples for Yersinia pestis, Mycobacterium tuberculosis and Tannerella forsythia; delivered count tables, figures and a comparative report.
12/2025 – 01/2026 EEHAR-CSIC (Escuela Española de Historia y Arqueología en Roma)

Osteological Laboratory, Tusculum Project

  • Catalogued and analysed medieval human remains from the Area G cathedral site (DG-ABAP no. 245): skeletal inventory and element identification, biological profile, and macroscopic recording of preservation, weathering and taphonomic alteration.
  • Managed the digitisation of the anthropological dataset, ensuring traceability between remains, records and photographic documentation.
07/2025 – 12/2025 Sapienza University of Rome

Research Internship at the Palaeoanthropology and Bioarchaeology Laboratory

  • Bioanthropological study and critical re-evaluation of a human cranial collection acquired by Giuseppe Sergi (early 20th century): biological profile, dental palaeopathology, history of previous study, and systematic assessment of the state of conservation, distinguishing taphonomic damage from damage introduced during excavation and curation. The objective was to revitalise a neglected scientific heritage collection and propose new avenues for research.
  • Designed the reproducible R pipeline used for the collection (Etruscan-Analysis, released open source).
04/2024 – 12/2024 ENEA Casaccia

Research Internship at the ENEA Research Centre, Casaccia (Rome)

  • Designed and executed independently an end-to-end metagenomics workflow on microbially mediated deterioration of a mineral substrate: field sampling and photographic documentation of biofilms on the historic walls of the Casino del Bel Respiro, genomic DNA extraction, PCR (16S rRNA, ITS), amplicon sequencing and phylogenetic classification.
  • Built the R pipeline from scratch (phyloseq, vegan, ggplot2): alpha- and beta-diversity, taxonomic composition and functional-group succession under sustainable conservation treatments. Presented as an oral contribution (UniPi, 2025) and a co-authored abstract.
2022 – 2024 Sapienza University of Rome

Research Internship: Taxonomic Identification and Iconographic Analysis

  • Study of The Four Elements cycle by Jan Brueghel the Elder: bibliographic research, taxonomic classification of the species represented, and high-resolution photographic documentation within the 17th-century natural-history context.
